Expert routing

Position p predicts token p + 1. The lens at the last token is the distribution over what the model writes next.
Mass is raw, never renormalised. The dashed line is the total probability on the label-opening tokens over the full vocabulary. Near zero means the model is not choosing among the labels at all, whatever their ratio to each other says.
Early depths are read through a head trained on the last one. A dark lens in the first layers means the residual is not yet in the output head's language, not that nothing is being computed.
