LFM2.5 Expert Contrast

Two ways of asking which of each layer's 32 experts matter to a verdict. The usage contrasts show which experts travel with a verdict across 733 judged commands. The knockout view removes experts one at a time and shows which carry it. They disagree, and the second is the one to believe.
